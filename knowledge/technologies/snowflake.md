# Snowflake

Overview
- Cloud warehouse platform for governed analytics and sharing.

Why I use it
- Useful when warehouse simplicity and sharing matter most.

When I choose it
- SQL-centric analytics
- Separation of storage and compute

When I avoid it
- Spark-native engineering workflows

Advantages
- Low operational overhead
- Elastic compute

Disadvantages
- Cost can climb without warehouse discipline

Alternatives
- Databricks
- BigQuery
- Redshift

Enterprise considerations
- Governance and cost management are important.

Scaling considerations
- Workload patterns and clustering drive efficiency.

Common mistakes
- Leaving compute overprovisioned

Best practices
- Match warehouse sizing to actual concurrency.

Related technologies
- SQL, Databricks
