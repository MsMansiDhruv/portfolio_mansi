# Engineering Principles

How I design systems
- Start with business outcomes and operating constraints.

How I balance delivery with quality
- Choose the smallest durable design that meets the need.

How I think about scalability
- Scale should be planned in data flow, ownership, and operations.

How I think about observability
- If a system cannot be observed, it cannot be trusted.

How I approach automation
- Automate repeated work and dangerous work first.

How I approach governance
- Make governance visible, explicit, and hard to bypass.
