# Leadership

How I lead
- Set technical standards without blocking delivery.
- Explain trade-offs so teams can make durable decisions.
- Keep business impact visible in architecture discussions.

Business outcomes
- Increase trust in platform decisions.
- Reduce rework by clarifying ownership.
- Help teams ship with less operational risk.
